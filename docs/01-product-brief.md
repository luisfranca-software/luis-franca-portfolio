\# 5\. Product Context

The Site Portfolio project aims to establish a professional digital platform representing Luís Eduardo Carvalho França as a Software Engineer.

The platform shall demonstrate engineering excellence through both its content and its own implementation quality.

Rather than functioning solely as a portfolio website, the platform is intended to become the foundation for a long-term professional ecosystem capable of evolving without architectural disruption.

The product itself shall serve as evidence of engineering capability by exhibiting professional standards in:

\- software engineering;  
\- architecture;  
\- maintainability;  
\- usability;  
\- accessibility;  
\- documentation quality;  
\- long-term evolution.

\---

\# 6\. Executive Summary

The primary objective of the Site Portfolio project is to create a professional portfolio platform capable of strengthening personal branding while demonstrating software engineering excellence.

The product shall prioritize:

\- professional credibility;  
\- engineering quality;  
\- simplicity;  
\- maintainability;  
\- long-term scalability.

Business value shall always take precedence over feature quantity.

Every future capability shall originate from an approved engineering specification in accordance with the Specification-Driven Development lifecycle.

\# 7\. Product Vision

The long-term vision is to establish a professional engineering platform that continuously supports career growth, business opportunities, and technical credibility.

The platform shall evolve incrementally while preserving:

\- architectural consistency;

\- maintainability.
